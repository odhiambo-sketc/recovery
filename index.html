Here is how it breaks down:

Step 1: The Client-List Audit

We start with your compliance-only clients: current clients who file with you once a year and buy nothing else. (Lapsed clients—no filing in 12+ months—are a second list with a different message). I audit this list to identify who looks ready for more based on their last filing date, entity type, and current services.

  • I do: All the data segmentation, list scrubbing, and opportunity analysis.
  • You do: Hand over a secure export of your past client list. That's it.

Step 2: Check-ins in Your Voice

I draft and install five short email sequences sent directly under your name. These notes are written specifically for accountants — calm, professional language that reads like a thoughtful 1-on-1 advisor note rather than a marketing broadcast. They highlight fiscal checkpoints, upcoming tax law shifts, or proactive health checks.

  • I do: Write 100% of the copy, set up the delivery triggers, and connect it to your existing tools (Xero, QuickBooks, Mailchimp, or direct SMTP).
  • You do: Review and approve the copy in a single 20-minute call.

Step 3: Booked Conversations

When dormant clients receive these structured check-ins, they don't feel "sold to" — they feel cared for. The sequences guide interested clients directly to your calendar to book an "Annual Review Call" or select pre-packaged advisory tiers (Quarterly Tax Planning, Fractional CFO, or Monthly Bookkeeping upgrades).

  • I do: Manage the calendar integration and tracking architecture.
  • You do: Conduct standard advisory reviews and collect monthly retainer fees.

I sign a confidentiality agreement before touching your data. The export is stored securely, used only for this build, and deleted the moment the sequences are approved. You approve everything that goes out; I don't claim to ensure compliance — that remains your firm's responsibility.
