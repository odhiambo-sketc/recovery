1. The Client-List Audit

I go through the client export you send me and sort your clients into two lists.

  • Compliance-only clients. Current clients who file with you once a year and buy nothing else. This is the main list.
  • Lapsed clients. No filing with you in the past 12–36 months. A smaller second list, with a different message.

I work from what's in your export.

Last filing date. Services bought. Fees billed. Entity type. Years with you.

From that I flag the clients most likely to be a fit for planning or advisory work.

I can't see inside a client's business, so you make the final call on who's on the list.

  • I do: the sorting, list cleanup, and opportunity flags.
  • You do: send a secure export of your client list, and review the final list before anything goes out.

2. Check-Ins in Your Voice

I write five short email sequences in your voice, sent from your address.

They read like a note from a thoughtful advisor, not a marketing broadcast.
